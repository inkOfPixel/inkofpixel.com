      This page explains what inkofpixel.com stores on your device when you visit it.

      In short: the site sets no cookies, which is why you see no banner asking for your consent.

      ## Cookies

      The site sets no cookies, neither its own nor from third parties. It carries no advertising, no profiling tools, no social network buttons and no content embedded from other platforms.

      The only exception we cannot rule out: Cloudflare, the network that delivers and protects the site, may set a strictly technical security cookie when it has to check that a request does not come from an automated program. It is not used to recognise or follow you.

      ## What is saved in your browser

      One thing only: the theme you chose. If you press the switch between the light and the dark theme, your choice is saved in the local storage of your browser under the name "theme", so the site looks the same on your next visit.

      It is saved only if you use the switch, it stays on your device and it is never sent to us. Since it is a preference you asked for yourself, it does not require consent.

      ## Visit statistics

      We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains what it measures.

      ## How to delete what is saved

      You can delete the saved theme at any time from the settings of your browser, by clearing the data of this site. The site keeps working exactly as before and goes back to the theme of your device.
