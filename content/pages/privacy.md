      This page explains which personal data we handle when you visit inkofpixel.com or write to us, and what you can do about it.

      In short: this site sets no cookies, shows no advertising and does not follow you from one site to another. We count visits in an anonymous, aggregate form, and we handle your personal data only when you write to us.

      ## Who is responsible for your data

      The data controller is **inkOfPixel Srl**, Piazza Castello 26, 20121 Milano (MI), Italy, VAT number 09287730965.

      For any question about this page or about your data, write to [hello@inkofpixel.com](mailto:hello@inkofpixel.com).

      ## Which data we handle, and why

      ### When you write to us

      The contact form asks for your name, your email address and your message. We need all three to reply to you; nothing else is requested.

      The message is forwarded to our mailbox by email and is not stored on the site. We use it only to reply to you and to follow up on your request. The same goes for the emails you send us directly.

      Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR).

      To keep the form from being abused, the server holds the IP address a message comes from in temporary memory, without writing it to disk, and uses it only to limit how many messages can be sent in a short time.

      ### When you browse the site

      Like every web server, ours and the network in front of it record a few technical details of each request: IP address, date and time, the page requested and the type of browser. We use them only to keep the site working and secure, never to identify or profile visitors.

      Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the security and operation of the site (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

      ### Visit statistics

      To understand how the site is used we rely on Cloudflare Web Analytics. A small script loaded from Cloudflare reports, in aggregate form, which pages are viewed, which site a visit comes from, the country, the type of device, browser and operating system, and how fast the pages load.

      It uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not recognise you from one visit to the next or across other sites. We only see totals: we cannot trace any of these numbers back to a person.

      Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used and in improving it (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

      ### Cookies and browser storage

      The site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or profiling tools. Fonts, images and scripts are served from our own domain, with the only exception of the statistics script described above.

      If you switch between the light and the dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser (local storage) so the site looks the same on your next visit. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

      ## Who receives your data

      We do not sell your data and we do not pass it on for marketing. It is handled by the people at inkOfPixel who deal with your request and by the suppliers that make the site and our email work, acting as data processors:

      - **Hetzner Online GmbH** (Germany): the server that hosts the site, located in Finland.
      - **Cloudflare, Inc.** (United States): the network that delivers and protects the site, and the visit statistics.
      - **Amazon Web Services** (United States): the email service that forwards the messages of the contact form.
      - **Google** (Google Workspace): the mailbox where we receive and answer messages.

      Some of these suppliers may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. In that case the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

      ## How long we keep it

      - **Messages**: for as long as it takes to answer you and follow up on your request. If we end up working together, for the duration of the relationship and for the periods the law requires.
      - **Technical records**: for a limited time; they are overwritten automatically.

      ## Your rights

      You can ask us at any time to see the data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to restrict its use, to receive a copy in a common format, or you can object to its use. Write to [hello@inkofpixel.com](mailto:hello@inkofpixel.com): we answer within one month.

      We make no automated decisions about you and we do no profiling.

      If you think we are handling your data incorrectly, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it), or with the authority of the country where you live.

      ## Links to other sites

      The site links to other websites, for example our clients' and our social profiles. Once you are there, their own privacy policies apply.

      ## Changes to this page

      If what the site does with data changes, we update this page.
