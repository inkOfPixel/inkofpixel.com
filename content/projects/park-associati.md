      Park Associati is an architecture practice based in Milan. We have been developing their website since 2021, and in 2025 we rebuilt it from scratch.

      ## A site rebuilt in 2025

      The first version dates back to 2021. In April 2025 we put the new site online, rebuilt with Remix and Tailwind CSS. The content is managed in a headless CMS, so the Park team publishes projects and news on its own, with a preview before going live.

      ![The home page of parkassociati.com](/assets/park-associati-home.jpg)

      ## The project archive

      The heart of the site is the archive of the practice's projects. It can be searched by name, even with a typo, filtered by category, and viewed as a grid of images or as a table.

      ![The project archive](/assets/park-associati-projects.jpg)

      ![A project page](/assets/park-associati-project.jpg)

      ## Italian and English

      Every page exists in both languages, each with its own address: "progetti" and "projects", "editoriale" and "editorial".

      ## Work with us

      Applications arrive through a form on the site, with CV and portfolio attached. Large files are uploaded directly to storage, so they never slow the site down.

      ## Found by search engines

      With the new site we took care of what search engines read: sitemap, structured data, and a clean-up of old addresses that should no longer be indexed.

      ## Results

      More than five years together, and two versions of the site. Today Park has a fast website that its team updates in complete autonomy.
