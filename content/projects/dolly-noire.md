      Dolly Noire is a streetwear brand born in Milan. Since 2022 we have been their technical partner for e-commerce: we rebuilt the Shopify theme and we have kept evolving it together with their team ever since.

      ## A custom, lightweight theme

      We started from Dawn, Shopify's reference theme, and rebuilt it around Dolly Noire's needs with Liquid, Alpine.js, Tailwind CSS and TypeScript.

      Collection filters work even without JavaScript and get richer when it is available, and the product grid loads more results as you scroll. The cart drawer shows the progress towards the free gift and suggests a product to add.

      ![A collection on the Dolly Noire website](/assets/dolly-noire-collection.jpg)

      Over time we also removed the third-party tools that were no longer needed, to keep pages fast.

      ![The same collection on a phone](/assets/dolly-noire-mobile.jpg)

      ## The right size, first time

      In fashion, picking the size is the main doubt before buying. We built a calculator that suggests a size from height and weight, right on the product page.

      We recently updated it: it now suggests only the sizes that the product actually comes in, and it recognises the larger sizes too.

      ![A product page with the size selector and the size guide](/assets/dolly-noire-product.jpg)

      ## A community connected to the store

      Dolly Noire has a very active community on Discord. We developed a custom Shopify app that links the customer account to Discord: customers connect from their account page, and their roles on the server update automatically according to their profile. Those who connect their account earn points in the loyalty programme.

      The same app prepares recurring customer reports for the team, with no manual exports. It is built with Remix, PostgreSQL and background jobs that keep everything in sync.

      ## Prices and products reserved for members

      Members of the Dolly Noire "Family" get dedicated prices and access to reserved products. We built the discount logic with Shopify Functions, so Shopify applies it at checkout with no code to type in, and the theme shows the reserved price on product cards and product pages.

      ## Verified accessibility

      In 2026 we worked on the accessibility of the site starting from a WCAG audit: accessible names for buttons, fields and links, correct form labels, a link to skip to the content. After the fixes we ran the scan again, to check that the errors were really gone.

      ## Results

      A collaboration that started in 2022 and continues today. Dolly Noire has a store that its team manages on its own from the Shopify editor, while we take care of the custom features: community, loyalty, sizing, accessibility. Not a project delivered and closed, but a product that grows together with the brand.

      Nell'abbigliamento la scelta della taglia è il dubbio principale prima dell'acquisto. Abbiamo costruito un calcolatore che suggerisce la taglia a partire da altezza e peso, direttamente nella pagina prodotto.

      Dolly Noire ha una community molto attiva su Discord. Abbiamo sviluppato un'app Shopify su misura che collega l'account cliente a Discord: il cliente si connette dalla propria area personale e i suoi ruoli sul server si aggiornano in automatico in base al profilo. Chi collega l'account riceve punti nel programma fedeltà.

      I membri della "Family" di Dolly Noire hanno prezzi dedicati e accesso a prodotti riservati. Abbiamo realizzato la logica degli sconti con Shopify Functions, così è Shopify ad applicarla al checkout senza codici da inserire, e il tema mostra il prezzo riservato nelle card e nella pagina prodotto.

Dopo le correzioni abbiamo ripetuto la scansione, per verificare che gli errori fossero davvero spariti.

Dolly Noire ha un e-commerce che il suo team gestisce in autonomia dall'editor di Shopify, mentre noi ci occupiamo delle funzionalità su misura: community, fedeltà, taglie, accessibilità. Non un progetto consegnato e chiuso, ma un prodotto che cresce insieme al brand.
