      SIXS makes technical sportswear: base layers and protective clothing for motorcycling, cycling and outdoor sports. Since 2024 we have been building and evolving their Shopify store.

      ## A custom theme

      We built the theme on our own base, derived from Shopify's Dawn: Liquid, Alpine.js, Tailwind CSS and TypeScript, with a cart written in React. It serves two stores, one of them dedicated to Poland.

      ## The right size

      Technical clothing must fit perfectly. On the product page a calculator suggests the size from height and weight, next to the size table of each product. Choosing a colour also updates the photo gallery.

      ## Finding a shop

      SIXS is also sold by retailers. We built the store locator, with a map, address search and directions, and a separate page for distributors.

      ## Two warehouses

      With stock in Italy and in Poland, a product can be sold out in one country and available in the other. The Polish store tells the customer when this happens, instead of just showing "sold out".

      ## Gifts and content

      In the cart customers can add gift wrapping or send a gift card to another person. The magazine articles, written on another platform, appear inside the store, and the reviews of the same product in its different versions are shown together.

      ## Results

      A collaboration that started in 2024 and continues today. SIXS manages content on its own from the Shopify editor, while we take care of the custom features.
