      Koala Babycare designs products that make life easier for mums and babies: baby carriers, pillows, muslins. From 2021 to 2023 we built their Shopify theme and kept it growing.

      ## A custom theme

      We built the theme on our own base, derived from Shopify's Dawn: Liquid, Alpine.js and Tailwind CSS, with a cart written in React. The cart shows the progress towards the free gifts and suggests products to add.

      ![The home page of koalababycare.com](/assets/koala-home.jpg)

      ## Bundles

      Koala's products are often bought together. We built a page where customers put together their own bundle, and the templates for the ready-made ones.

      ## Product pages that answer questions

      Parents want to be sure before they buy. The product page has dedicated sections for tutorials, frequent questions, comparison between models and the opinions of experts, alongside customer reviews.

      ![A product page](/assets/koala-product.jpg)

      ## After the purchase

      We also took care of what comes after the order: product registration, the page with the manuals to download, a shipping cost calculator and the choice of country.

      ![The page with the product manuals](/assets/koala-manuals.jpg)

      ## Results

      Two years of work side by side with the Koala team, on a store translated into five languages and updated every week. A solid base that the brand kept building on.
