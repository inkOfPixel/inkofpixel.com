      STIMM, Shopping Therapy In My Mind, started as a shoe shop in Pieve di Soligo, in the Prosecco hills, and is now a group of multi-brand concept stores. We have worked with them since 2016: first the move from Magento to Shopify, then, in 2025, a store rebuilt from scratch.

      > Ironic approach to fashion. Real contemporary style. Therapy of my moods.

      ## Chapter one: from Magento to Shopify

      STIMM needed a platform that would help them grow into a true multi-channel business. Their old Magento e-commerce was slow, hard to navigate and expensive to run. We proposed Shopify, which also covers sales in the physical shops, and we moved the whole store over.

      The result was a faster website, a quicker and more useful admin panel, and a clearer view of how the store was doing. Together with STIMM we also redesigned the experience: product pages that show quality and details, and a fast search across a catalogue of several thousand products.

      ![The men section of the first Shopify site](/assets/inkofpixel_stimm_man_page.webp)

      ## Chapter two: a store rebuilt in 2025

      After years of growth, in 2025 we rebuilt the store from scratch. The new theme starts from Shopify's Dawn and is written with Stimulus, Turbo and Tailwind CSS, with no heavy build step: less code, pages that load faster, and a base that is easier to evolve.

      Search and filters now run on Shopify's own engine, with instant suggestions while typing.

      ![The home page of the new stimm.com](/assets/stimm-home.jpg)

      ## Outfits you can shop

      STIMM does not sell single garments, it builds looks. On the outfit pages each look is a photo with its items marked on it: the customer sees the complete outfit and adds what they like to the cart. To prepare them, the STIMM team uses a small tool we built that turns clicks on the photo into the position of each item.

      ## Hundreds of brands, easy to find

      A multi-brand store lives on its brands. The brand index lists them from A to Z, separately for women and men, and takes the customer straight to the right collection.

      ![The index of the women brands](/assets/stimm-brands.jpg)

      ## Product page and wishlist

      The size chart opens next to the product without leaving the page, related items complete the look, and customers can save their favourites in a wishlist or give a gift card.

      ## The physical shops

      STIMM's shops are part of the experience, starting from the one in Belluno, inside a former bank building. Each shop has its own page on the site.

      ![The page of the Belluno shop](/assets/stimm-shop.jpg)

      ## Results

      Ten years together and two generations of the store: first the migration from Magento, then a rebuild that made the site lighter and closer to the way STIMM sells, by outfits and by brands. The STIMM team manages content, outfits and promotions on its own.

      ## Outfit da acquistare

      STIMM non vende singoli capi, costruisce look. Nelle pagine outfit ogni look è una foto con i capi indicati sopra: il cliente vede l'outfit completo e aggiunge al carrello ciò che gli piace. Per prepararli, il team STIMM usa un piccolo strumento realizzato da noi, che trasforma i clic sulla foto nella posizione di ogni capo.

      ## Centinaia di brand, facili da trovare

      Un multibrand vive dei suoi marchi. L'indice dei brand li elenca dalla A alla Z, separati tra donna e uomo, e porta il cliente direttamente alla collezione giusta.

      ## Pagina prodotto e preferiti

      La tabella taglie si apre accanto al prodotto senza lasciare la pagina, i capi correlati completano il look, e il cliente può salvare i preferiti in una lista dei desideri o regalare una gift card.

      ## I negozi fisici

      I negozi STIMM sono parte dell'esperienza, a partire da quello di Belluno, dentro un ex palazzo bancario. Ogni negozio ha la sua pagina sul sito.
